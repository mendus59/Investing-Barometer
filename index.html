    How it works

    Every day a GitHub Actions job downloads the latest values from FRED, the St. Louis Fed's free economic database. Each category is scored on a few factors. Each factor adds +1 (favorable), 0 (neutral) or −1 (unfavorable).

    • A total of +2 or more is Buy (or Overweight for cash).
    • A total of −2 or less is Sell (or Underweight).
    • Anything in between is Hold. With fewer than two factors available, there's No signal.

    Signals are about broad index funds for each asset class, never individual stocks or bonds. For housing, the view is that of someone buying a home to live in: Buy means conditions favor buyers and Sell means a seller's market. ZIP code data comes from Zillow (home values and rents, monthly) and the Census Bureau (median household income), and is refreshed weekly.

    The rules are intentionally simple and transparent. You can read them all in scripts/update.py.

    Data: Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (FRED), with series from Freddie Mac, the Federal Reserve Board, U.S. Treasury, S&P Dow Jones Indices, Nikkei, Cboe, ICE BofA, Coinbase, U.S. Census Bureau, BLS and EIA. ZIP code home values and rents: Zillow Home Value Index and Zillow Observed Rent Index. Educational use only. Not financial advice.
