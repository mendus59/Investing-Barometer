How it works

Every day a GitHub Actions job downloads the latest values from FRED, the St. Louis Fed's free economic database. Each category is scored on a few factors. Each factor adds +1 (favorable), 0 (neutral) or −1 (unfavorable).

  • A total of +2 or more is Buy (or Overweight for cash).
  • A total of −2 or less is Sell (or Underweight).
  • Anything in between is Hold.

For housing, the view is that of someone buying a home to live in. Buy means conditions favor buyers; Sell means a seller's market. Some series (home prices, income, consumer loan rates) are published monthly, quarterly or yearly, so their "as of" dates lag.

The rules are intentionally simple and transparent. You can read them all in scripts/update.py.

Data: Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (FRED), with series from Freddie Mac, the Federal Reserve Board, U.S. Treasury, S&P Dow Jones Indices, Cboe, ICE BofA, U.S. Census Bureau and BLS. Educational use only. Not financial advice.
